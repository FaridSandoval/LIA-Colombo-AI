There are many different types of community. One type is 
community of place. The place could be a neighborhood, 
a region, or a country. A second is community of interest.

is a group of people who share the same profession.
Each type of community can offer different things to its 
members. A community of place might offer people to chat 
with and to support you in difﬁcult times. A community of 
interest can give you friendship and a way to learn more 
about a subject. A community of purpose can help you learn 
ways of achieving your goals. Also, people working together 
can often achieve more than a person working by himself or 
herself. A community of practice gives you opportunities to 
share ideas as well as the chance to network.

of community. For example, a local cycling club is a 
community of place and a community of interest. If the

One tool to help planners make a decision is a decision

each plan against each result: –2 for a very negative effect, 
–1 for a negative effect, 0 for no effect, +1 for a positive

People can use a decision matrix to help with all kinds
