We humans use a lot of materials.Take plastic, 
for example.

According to Statista, every year, around 350 million 
metric tons of plastic are produced around the world.
Sadly, not much of it is recycled. In fact, about half 
of the plastic is used just once and is then sent to a
landﬁll, according to Plastic Oceans International. In 
2007, Charles Fishman of Fast Company Magazine
reported that a million plastic bottles are bought every
minute of every day in the US, but only around 23% 
are recycled. Four trillion plastic bags are produced
each year, and only 1% are recycled, according to
Waste Management. Our use of other plastics is not
much better. Plastic use is a big problem.

Many people are looking at ways to upcycle different
materials. Upcycling involves ﬁnding new uses for 
materials at the end of their original use. In one 
project, a greenhouse was created using old plastic
water bottles. In others, large coffee cans or old
rubber boots were turned into containers for plants, 
and old rubber tires became notebook covers. Artists,
too, upcycle materials. Tim Noble and Sue Webster,
two British artists, create what look like piles of trash. 
When a light shines on the trash from a certain angle, 
it makes an interesting shadow on the wall.

These days institutions such as colleges and
companies are looking for smarter ways to 
use materials. This involves reducing the amount of 
materials they use and thinking of ways to upcycle 
materials so that they last a long time.

Alternatively, direct students to research sites that give them 
ideas for upcycling by typing into a search engine “ways to 
upcycle plastic bottles” and “ways to upcycle cardboard 
boxes,” and ask them to choose the best project.
