# Unit 9 — Room for Me

## Lesson 1 — Housing Solutions

### Vocabulary
<!-- audience: student -->
<!-- topic: vocabulary -->

**Housing Solutions**
Read the definitions to learn vocabulary related to different ways of living and homes:
- **tiny house:** a very small house, usually built to maximize space and minimize cost.
- **eco-friendly:** not harmful to the environment.
- **neighborhood:** the area of a town that surrounds someone's home.
- **space:** an empty area that is available to be used.
- **community:** the people living in one particular area.
- **affordable:** not expensive; cheap enough for most people to buy or rent.

### Grammar
<!-- audience: student -->
<!-- topic: grammar -->
<!-- grammar_focus: future_forms -->

**Future Forms**
We use different forms to talk about the future depending on our meaning:
- **Predictions:** We use *will* and *going to* to make predictions. (*I think tiny houses will be very popular.*)
- **Intentions:** We use *going to* to talk about future plans or intentions. (*I am going to move to a new apartment next year.*)
- **Arrangements:** We use the *present progressive* to talk about fixed future arrangements. (*We are meeting the real estate agent tomorrow at 10 a.m.*)
- **Schedules:** We use the *simple present* to talk about future events that happen on a fixed schedule. (*The moving truck arrives at 8 a.m.*)

### Practice
<!-- audience: student -->
<!-- topic: practice -->

1. Choose the correct future form to complete the sentences:
   a. I can't meet you tomorrow because I (am visiting / visit) a new apartment with my roommate.
   b. Look at those dark clouds! It (is going to rain / rains) soon.
   c. The train to the new neighborhood (leaves / is leaving) at 6:30 p.m. every evening.
   d. I have decided that I (am going to live / live) in an eco-friendly community after graduation.

### Teacher Notes
<!-- audience: teacher_only -->
<!-- topic: pedagogy -->

**Vocabulary:** Ask students to discuss the pros and cons of living in a "tiny house". Have them use the vocabulary words (e.g., *Is it affordable? Is there enough space?*).
**Grammar Activity:** Write the four future forms on the board. Say a sentence out loud and have students shout out whether it is a Prediction, Intention, Arrangement, or Schedule.
**Speaking:** Have students discuss housing solutions. Where do they think most people will live in 50 years?

### Answer Key
<!-- audience: teacher_only -->
<!-- topic: answer_key -->

**Practice (Future Forms):**
a. am visiting (Arrangement)
b. is going to rain (Prediction based on evidence)
c. leaves (Schedule)
d. am going to live (Intention)

### Cultural Notes
<!-- audience: teacher_only -->
<!-- topic: cultural_context -->

The "Tiny House Movement" has become very popular in countries like the US, Canada, and Australia as a response to expensive housing markets and a desire to live more sustainably. Exploring this trend is a great way to discuss affordable housing solutions.

## Lesson 2 — Living Together

### Vocabulary
<!-- audience: student -->
<!-- topic: vocabulary -->

**Living Together**
Words and phrases to describe sharing a home with others:
- **roommate / flatmate:** a person who you share an apartment or house with.
- **rent:** the amount of money you pay regularly to live in a house or apartment.
- **share chores:** to divide the housework (like cleaning, taking out the trash) between the people living together.
- **compromise:** an agreement reached where everyone gets part of what they want.
- **privacy:** the state of being alone and not watched or disturbed by other people.

### Grammar
<!-- audience: student -->
<!-- topic: grammar -->
<!-- grammar_focus: future_possibility_certainty -->

**Future Possibility and Certainty**
We use different modals and adverbs to show how sure we are about the future:
- **100% Certain:** *will*, *won't*, *will definitely*, *definitely won't*.
  *Example:* I **will definitely** share chores with my roommate.
- **50% Possible:** *may*, *might*, *could*.
  *Example:* We **might** rent a larger apartment next year. (It's possible, but not certain).
- **Probably:** *will probably*, *probably won't*.
  *Example:* I **will probably** live with my parents until I graduate.

### Practice
<!-- audience: student -->
<!-- topic: practice -->

1. Rewrite the sentences using the word in parentheses to show possibility or certainty:
   a. I am 100% sure I will get a roommate. (definitely)
      I ______________________________ get a roommate.
   b. It is possible that she will move to a new city. (might)
      She ______________________________ to a new city.
   c. I am quite sure we won't buy a house this year. (probably)
      We ______________________________ a house this year.

### Teacher Notes
<!-- audience: teacher_only -->
<!-- topic: pedagogy -->

**Vocabulary:** Brainstorm common rules for roommates. What are the most important chores to share? Why is privacy important? 
**Grammar:** Emphasize the position of *definitely* and *probably*. They usually go *after* "will" but *before* "won't" (e.g., *will definitely*, *definitely won't*).
**Listening:** Have students listen to a conversation about two friends deciding where to live. Have them listen for the speakers' attitudes towards sharing space and paying rent.

### Answer Key
<!-- audience: teacher_only -->
<!-- topic: answer_key -->

**Practice (Future Possibility and Certainty):**
a. I will definitely get a roommate.
b. She might move to a new city.
c. We probably won't buy a house this year.

### Cultural Notes
<!-- audience: teacher_only -->
<!-- topic: cultural_context -->

In many Western countries, it is a very common rite of passage for young adults to move out of their parents' homes and live with roommates during or immediately after college. In other cultures, multi-generational living is the norm until marriage.

## Lesson 3 — Future Homes and Appliances

### Vocabulary
<!-- audience: student -->
<!-- topic: vocabulary -->

**Appliances and the Home of the Future**
Words for modern equipment and features in a house:
- **appliance:** a piece of electrical equipment with a particular purpose in the home.
- **smart device:** a device that connects to the internet to be controlled remotely.
- **solar panels:** equipment on a roof that uses light energy from the sun to produce electricity.
- **voice-controlled:** operated by spoken commands.
- **energy-efficient:** using very little energy to provide power.

### Grammar
<!-- audience: student -->
<!-- topic: grammar -->
<!-- grammar_focus: writing_opinion_paragraph -->

*(Note: In this lesson, the focus shifts towards Writing and Speaking about the future using the target vocabulary).*

**Writing an Opinion Paragraph**
When writing an opinion paragraph about the future, state your prediction clearly and support it with reasons:
- *In my opinion, homes in the future will be 100% energy-efficient.*
- *First of all, solar panels will be on every roof.*
- *Furthermore, voice-controlled appliances will help us save time.*

### Practice
<!-- audience: student -->
<!-- topic: practice -->

1. Match the appliance or feature to its description:
   a. solar panels
   b. smart device
   c. energy-efficient appliance
   d. voice-controlled system
   
   1. A refrigerator that uses 50% less electricity. _____
   2. A machine that creates energy from the sun. _____
   3. A speaker that plays music when you tell it to. _____
   4. A thermostat you can adjust using your phone from work. _____

### Teacher Notes
<!-- audience: teacher_only -->
<!-- topic: pedagogy -->

**Vocabulary:** Discuss what appliances students currently have in their homes that are "smart." What appliance do they wish was invented?
**Writing:** Have students write a short opinion paragraph about how homes will change in the next 20 years. Remind them to use future forms for predictions (*will / are going to*).
**Language and Life - Decision-making:** Have students work in groups to use a "Decision Matrix" to plan and develop a new eco-friendly neighborhood, deciding which features are most important (e.g., solar panels vs. large green spaces).

### Answer Key
<!-- audience: teacher_only -->
<!-- topic: answer_key -->

**Practice (Appliances):**
1. c
2. a
3. d
4. b

### Cultural Notes
<!-- audience: teacher_only -->
<!-- topic: cultural_context -->

The integration of "smart" technology into everyday appliances is changing household dynamics globally. Discussions around privacy (e.g., voice-controlled devices listening to conversations) are very relevant when talking about the "home of the future."
