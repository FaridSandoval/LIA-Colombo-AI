MINDFULNESS

RELAXATION

Study, work, family, friends, pets, social media … We feel tired 
and stressed because we can’t do everything. The effects of this 
are that we are less efficient, less creative, and less happy. We all try 
to do leisure activities like playing sports or watching movies, but 
what about leisure time for the mind?

In recentyears, manypeople have started to practicemindfulness. 
Being mindful means that you are paying attention to what you are
doing at the moment, to your feelings, and to the things that are
happening around you. You are not thinking about things that have
already happened or worrying about what is going to happen. For 
example, if you notice how the sun feels on your face, or how your 
clean clothes smell, you are being mindful.

There are some simple things that you can do to practice
mindfulness. Do you like walking? Go for a walk, but don’t think 
about walking fast or going a certain distance. Walk slowly. Think 
about how your body feels. Stop and look at trees or flowers. Stop 
and close your eyes. What do you hear, feel, or smell? Would you
rraatther do something creative? Paint something or do a craft. Don’t 
worry if it isn’t good. Think about the process. How does the
paintbrush feel in your hand? What does each color make you 
tthink of? Giving your brain time to relax can help you feel less 
ssttressed and more rested. The result is more creativity and better 
pproblem solving.

There are many free mindfulness apps available for 
smartphones. Have students search for a selection and try 
out two or three. Then have them report back to the class 
on the kinds of activities the apps offer and whether they 
found them useful or not.
